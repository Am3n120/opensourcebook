Open Sourcebook Beta

The open-source NotebookLM (now Gemini Notebook) alternative with explainer videos.

Understand what you have to read. Explain what you know.

Preparing for an exam · reading a stack of papers · getting a new colleague up to speed · turning a manual into something people will watch.

Add your PDFs, notes, slides and links. Open Sourcebook turns them into a short explainer video, drawn scene by scene, and also makes guides, audio, flashcards, quizzes and answers with cites.

Mac and Linux

curl -fsSL https://opensourcebook.app/install | sh

Paste it into Terminal and press Return. Open Sourcebook opens in your browser after a few minutes.

Windows

irm https://opensourcebook.app/install.ps1 | iex

Paste it into PowerShell and press Enter. Open Sourcebook opens in your browser after a few minutes.

It runs on the AI plan you already have: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or GitHub Copilot. No API key is needed, and one works too.

Made and used on a Mac. Linux and Windows are written and not proven yet.

  • Explainer videos, drawn for your materialTwelve styles, any length up to 30 minutes, with captions, and narration and music once you set a voice.
  • The AI you already haveYour plan or an API key. Six agents, and any model they offer.
  • A whole set from one requestVideos, guides, audio, flashcards and quizzes in order, as a plan you can change before it starts.
  • Answers you can check, on your computerEvery cite is checked word for word. Your files stay there. Open source under MIT.
